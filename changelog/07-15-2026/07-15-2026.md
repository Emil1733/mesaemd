# Platform Changelog & SEO Execution: 2026-07-15

## 1. Google Search Console Analysis (30-Day Evaluation: Jun 15 - Jul 14)
We ran our latest 30-day comparative SEO audit to assess the impact of our previous internal linking structure.

### The Successes: Internal Links Worked
*   **Permit Hub Domination:** `maricopa county pool inspections` jumped from Pos 39.0 to **16.7** due to aggressive homepage linking.
*   **Caliche Relevance:** `caliche soil arizona` indexed natively at **Pos 22.0**.
*   **High-Intent "Near Me" Penetration:** `in ground pool removal near me` entered at **Pos 4.0** (generating a tracked click with 50% CTR). `pool removal contractor near me` climbed from 10.5 to **Pos 5.0**.

### The Problem: Core Mesa Keywords Stuck
*   `pool demolition mesa` remained flat at Pos 62.0.
*   `swimming pool removal mesa` actually dropped 6.5 positions to Pos 68.3.
*   *Diagnosis:* The site was caught in an algorithmic evaluation sandbox due to two main issues: **Keyword Cannibalization** (the Permit page was outranking the homepage for main terms) and **Orphaned Indexing** (Google only indexed 3 pages total out of 14, ignoring the neighborhood hubs due to perceived duplicate content/lack of canonicals).

---

## 2. Technical SEO & Codebase Executions to Shatter the Ceiling

### Fix A: Keyword De-Cannibalization (Permit Hub)
*   **Action:** Modified `PermitClient.tsx` to stop competing for core demolition terms.
*   **Implementation:** Injected exact-match anchor text pointing back to the homepage: *"If you are looking for actual **Mesa pool demolition services** or need a **swimming pool removal Mesa** quote, return to our main service page."*

### Fix B: Massive "Geo-Payload" Injection (Homepage)
*   **Action:** Updated `HomeClient.tsx` to drastically increase raw local semantic relevance for the city of Mesa.
*   **Implementation:** Added a 250+ word, highly dense structural block detailing Mesa-specific zip codes (85209, 85202, 85207, 85215), noise ordinances, HOA restrictions, and heavy equipment access considerations.

### Fix C: Canonical Tagging (Neighborhood Hubs)
*   **Action:** Addressed the indexation problem where Google classified neighborhood pages as duplicates of the homepage.
*   **Implementation:** Ran a programmatic script to inject explicit `alternates: { canonical: '/mesa/[neighborhood-name]' }` metadata into the `page.tsx` files for Dobson Ranch, Eastmark, Las Sendas, and Red Mountain Ranch.

### Fix D: Internal "Crawl Web" (Neighborhood Hubs)
*   **Action:** Eliminated the "Orphan Page" penalty.
*   **Implementation:** Injected a cross-linking component at the bottom of all neighborhood client files (`*Client.tsx`), permanently tying them together. This guarantees that when Google crawls one, it is forced to crawl and validate all of them simultaneously.
